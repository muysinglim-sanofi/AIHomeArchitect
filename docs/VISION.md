# Product Vision — AIHomeArchitect

> This is the emotional and philosophical foundation of the product. When making any design or implementation decision, check it against this file.

---

## What This Product Is

**AIHomeArchitect is an AI architect companion.**

Not an image filter. Not a Pinterest mood board. Not a random redesign generator.

It is a **believable, conversational design intelligence** that helps people understand what their space could become — and guides them there iteratively, like a real architect would.

The emotional goal in one sentence:

> **"This feels like talking to a real designer."**

---

## The Core Experience

A user takes a photo of their room. They don't need to describe anything, know any design vocabulary, or make any complex decisions. They simply share what they have.

The AI:
- reads the architecture of the space (proportions, light, structure)
- proposes a transformation that respects what is already there
- explains its decisions with the confidence of someone who has thought about it
- refines iteratively based on what the user responds to

Every response should feel considered, not generated. Every redesign should feel like it could exist in the real world.

## What This Product Is NOT

| Not this | Why |
|----------|-----|
| A Pinterest / mood board generator | We don't generate options — we develop a single coherent direction |
| A random AI redesign tool | Every output is atmosphere-specific, architecture-specific, and room-specific |
| A fantasy rendering engine | Results must be physically plausible — correct proportions, real materials, gravity-obeying furniture |
| A generic chatbot | The AI speaks as an architect, not a customer service assistant |

## Design Philosophy

### Preservation-first
The source space is not a canvas to paint over. It is architecture. The room's proportions, window positions, ceiling height, and footprint are fixed realities that the redesign must work with, not ignore.

Camera angle is never shifted. The user's actual space must be recognisable in the output.

### Realism-first
Every output must be physically believable. Furniture sits on floors. Curtains hang from ceilings. Materials have texture and weight. Light comes from sources that actually exist in the room.

The quality benchmark is **Architectural Digest** or **Wallpaper\*** — not a real estate CGI rendering or a stable diffusion output.

### Quiet luxury
The default aesthetic register is calm and premium. No flash, no excess, no novelty. The atmospheres are inspired by boutique hotels, private villas, and high-end residential architecture — spaces where design is felt rather than seen.

### Atmosphere consistency
A user who selects Japandi Calm should experience Japandi Calm across every room, every iteration, every follow-up. The atmosphere is a design language, not a mood filter. It has material logic, lighting logic, and furniture logic that must be coherent across visible spaces.

### Emotionally intelligent responses
The AI does not say "Great choice! Here's your redesign." It says something specific about what it prioritised, why it made certain decisions, and what direction is available next. It is confident, restrained, and expert.

## The Atmosphere System as Product Philosophy

The 10 atmospheres are not just prompt templates. They are **design identities**:
- Each has a philosophy that defines what the atmosphere fundamentally is
- Each has a luxury benchmark that defines the quality level
- Each has forbidden elements that define what it explicitly refuses to be
- Each has room-specific adaptations that make it coherent across an entire home

The atmosphere system is the product's design intelligence made tangible. Extending it (adding atmospheres, adding room types) is a significant product decision, not a technical task.

## The Conversational Architect Standard

Every AI response should pass this test:

**Would a confident, senior interior architect say this?**

A real architect:
- references specific decisions, not generic praise
- knows what they changed and why
- asks focused follow-up questions, not open-ended ones
- does not over-explain — they assume intelligence
- does not over-promise — they describe what was done, not what might be possible
- uses design vocabulary naturally, not performatively

The AI companion message is the product's voice. It should be short, specific, and confident.

## Long-Term Ambition

AIHomeArchitect is building toward a **persistent AI architect** — one that knows your home, understands your aesthetic evolution, and can guide a multi-room, multi-session design journey.

The foundation for this is already in place:
- Stateless refinement memory (rebuilt from conversation history)
- Atmosphere DNA (coherent identity across rooms)
- Visible spaces logic (multi-room scene intelligence)

Future waves build upward from this foundation. The product gets smarter as the conversation gets longer — not because it stores state, but because the conversation itself is the memory.
