## Executive summary

**3 root causes of WM's success identified :**

1. **Residential-scale architectural framing** — WM's `architectural_language` says "residential-scale spaces". Other atmospheres say "open-pavilion volumes" (Bali), "Open-plan volumes dissolving into landscape" (Tropical), "Floor-level living, garden views" (Zen). These are ARCHITECTURAL DIRECTIVES that drive the model toward changing the apartment to match. WM doesn't have this — it anchors to "this apartment as it is".

2. **Pure-style `forbidden_elements`** — WM's forbidden list is STYLE-only (cold minimalism, sterile white, glossy marble, fake gold, overdecorated styling). Other atmospheres include ARCHITECTURAL forbidden (Tropical "open-air kitsch", Zen "ultra modern tech minimalism", Bali "tiki bar"). Pure-style restrictions don't drive geometric change ; architectural restrictions do.

3. **LEAN `room_specific_constraints`** — WM has 2 constraints, both LEAN (conversation grouping + focal wall options). Other atmospheres pile on : density limits ("maximum 2 objects"), composition mandates ("symmetry"), focal mandates ("plant as primary accent"), spatial directives ("open side to garden"). Each adds spatial pressure.

**Proposed architecture (deliverable #10) :** Single canonical engine (WM-derived) + atmosphere overlays restricted to material/color/texture/decor — NOT spatial semantics.

**8 atmospheres for derivation** (Japandi + Zen opt-out per user) :
- Tropical Escape, Bali Sanctuary, Soft Luxury, Dark Contemporary, Nordic Warmth, Nature Retreat, Desert Luxe, Warm Modern (baseline)

# Section B — 10 Deliverables

## Deliverable 1 — Warm Modern Canonical Ruleset

The empirical canonical profile (post all cleanup waves) :

```
RULES (must be true for WM-derived atmospheres) :

R1. architectural_language anchors to "residential-scale spaces" or atmosphere-
    specific neutral equivalent. NEVER includes "open-plan", "open-pavilion",
    "dissolving", "indoor-outdoor flow", "garden views", "floor-level living".

R2. forbidden_elements are STYLE-ONLY (palette, hardware, materials, style
    clichés). NEVER includes architectural forbidden (e.g. "no open-pavilion",
    "no enclosed").

R3. room_specific_constraints are LEAN : maximum 2 items, both structural
    (seating arrangement, focal wall options). NEVER includes density caps
    ("maximum X objects"), composition mandates ("symmetry"), focal mandates
    ("X as primary accent"), or architectural directives ("open side to...").

R4. decor_language has MAX 2 items, ALL geometry-anchored (within seating
    footprint, at bedside, above existing vanity, along window lines, etc.).
    NEVER wall-occupying decor without anchor.

R5. negative_rules are STYLE-ONLY (palette, hardware, materials, style
    clichés). NEVER includes anti-furniture rules ("no TV", "no rug").

R6. focal-wall constraint EXPLICITLY lists television as one of the focal
    options (alongside fireplace/artwork/etc.).

R7. visible_transition_logic is CONTINUITY language (X echoes through visible
    Y), NEVER architectural directive ("open side to terrace").

R8. furniture_language is material/texture/form only (no named pieces with
    spatial implication).

R9. NO symmetry mandate without preserve-anchor (if symmetry desired, wording
    must be "where the photographed apartment allows symmetric placement").

R10. NO density caps ("maximum X furniture pieces / decorative objects") on
     derivable atmospheres. Density emerges from photo context naturally.
```

## Deliverable 2 — Why Warm Modern preserves better

3 root causes ranked :

**1. (Highest impact) architectural_language anchors to "residential-scale"** — doesn't instruct model to change the apartment's architectural character. Other atmospheres ("open-pavilion", "open-plan dissolving") instruct the model to make the apartment match the ideal. WM has no such ideal beyond "residential-scale, organic forms".

**2. forbidden_elements pure-style** — no architectural forbidden. The model isn't reminded of architectural NO-NOs that imply NEEDED YES-YES. Other atmospheres' architectural forbidden (Tropical "no enclosed", Bali "no boxy") implicitly push toward the opposite.

**3. room_specific_constraints LEAN** — only 2 items, both structural. No density caps to enforce (which the model interprets as "remove things"), no composition mandates (which the model interprets as "rearrange the apartment to comply").

## Deliverable 3 — Why Warm Modern produces TVs more naturally

**1. TV explicitly listed in focal-wall constraint** (post Wave 5.5.21) — `"single clear focal wall — fireplace, artwork, or a television, not multiple"`. The model has explicit permission.

**2. Vessel removed from decor_language** (Wave 5.5.21) — no wall-competing decor occupying the focal slot.

**3. No anti-TV negation in negative_rules** — "no chrome hardware" doesn't tell model to avoid TVs.

**4. No competing focal mandate** — atmosphere doesn't say "plant as primary accent" or "art wall is the focal".

**5. atmosphere identity ALLOWS TV** — boutique hotel / premium urban residence luxury level is TV-compatible (vs Japandi spareness, Zen anti-tech).
